# Package-Owned Human Credential Expiry Cleanup for v0.5.0

## Destination

Deliver complete package-owned discovery and direct expiry cleanup for human-user credential work left behind when
an application remains offline through grant expiry. A restarted consumer runner must use bounded package queries
and purpose-specific commands, not decrypt credentials, invoke providers or copy lifecycle policy. Invitation and
password reset require delivery terminalization; email change requires full authority and matching reservation expiry.

John approved this single destination and confirmed its complete boundary after
[WF-025](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-025-human-delivery-expiry-contract.md) resolved the family treatment and case matrix.
The [Wayfinder map](../wayfinder/human-credential-delivery-expiry-map.md) owns only this EPIC handoff. This capability is
a [v0.5.0 readiness prerequisite](../ROADMAP.md#human-delivery-expiry-readiness-prerequisite), not release authorization.
Planning approval is not implementation acceptance or proof that the external Agent OS integration blocker is resolved.

## Decisions so far

### D1 — Discover expired work without changing due discovery

Add bounded, deterministic, secret-free, read-only expired-work discovery through the three existing Domain grant
repositories and a package QueryHandler. Keep due discovery's existing eligibility and retry/lease policy unchanged.
Select authoritative latest issued invitation/reset generations with recoverable pending, retry-pending or claimed
delivery at exact expiry or later, independent of due/lease time. Select authoritative latest issued email-change
grants at grant expiry regardless of delivery status or recoverable material.

Filter eligibility before ordering/limiting; order globally by expiry instant, delivery ID, then purpose. Validate
limits of 1–100, document runner guidance of 50, and enforce the global bound after bounded family reads. Excluded
history and otherwise ineligible rows must not starve supported eligible work. Return immutable safe work sufficient
to dispatch the exact package command: purpose, User/delivery identity, the grant identity needed for email expiry,
expiry, revision and safe delivery status. Do not return credentials, material, digests, destinations, claim tokens or
provider errors. Queries never mutate, commit or publish facts.

### D2 — Invitation and password reset expire delivery

Add direct invitation delivery expiry with a post-commit fact; reuse and strengthen reset's existing direct expiry
command/handler and fact as necessary. Reuse aggregate `expireDeliveryAt()` under transactional repository validation.
Destroy recoverable ciphertext and claim token/time/lease atomically with delivery terminalization, preserving immutable
delivery identity, attempt count and last attempt/outcome/failure evidence. Already material-free terminal delivery
needs no new transition or success fact. Cleanup neither attempts delivery nor changes retry/backoff policy.

### D3 — Email change expires authority and its exact reservation

Reuse/adapt `ExpireEmailChange` and its handler, not a competing delivery-only cleanup workflow. At grant expiry,
atomically expire authoritative issued grant authority and clear only its exact matching User reservation. Recoverable
delivery is invalidated by the authority transition; already material-free terminal delivery retains its status/history.
Delivered, permanent-failure and previously delivery-expired work still requires authority/reservation cleanup.

Cleanup must work in every reachable User state, including disabled, deleted and restored-to-pending activation.
Narrowly adapt reservation expiry and its repository validation while preserving current account state, canonical email,
password, authentication/authorization authority and unrelated fields. Only reservation revision and normal update
metadata may change. No reactivation, canonical-email promotion or broader account-lifecycle behavior is authorized.

### D4 — Fence every transactional effect and preserve recovery evidence

Each direct command revalidates exact authoritative generation, ownership and expiry within its transaction. Preserve
complete-state compare-and-swap and stale-worker fencing. Both email grant and reservation changes share one transaction;
loss of either CAS cannot leave either half committed. Replaced, consumed, cancelled/revoked, fully expired or newer
state must not be revived or overwritten. Old cleanup and delayed provider outcomes cannot affect a successor, including
same-email reservation ABA. Repeats and ordinary stale/no-op commands do not write unchanged state or publish success facts.

Distinguish ordinary stale work from inconsistent persisted relationships: fail closed with safe unresolved-work evidence,
not fabricated cleanup success or implicit consumer lifecycle repair. No arbitrary corruption-reconciliation system is
included. Preserve ordinary command failure/rethrow and post-commit publication semantics; a publication fault does not
undo committed cleanup or guarantee later event delivery. Resolve uncertain commit/response loss from authoritative state
after restart, never from an assumed rollback or duplicate terminalization. No reliable-publication system is added.

Consumers protect every dispatch entry point and supply trusted actor provenance, exact work identities and time;
actor identity alone is not authorization. No decryption capability or provider collaborator is required by cleanup.

## Boundaries and exclusions

Production remains framework-neutral Domain/Application. Consumers own production persistence adapters, SQL/DDL,
transport, scheduler composition, keys, providers and runtime qualification. This EPIC changes public repository
contracts and documents consumer obligations, but implements no production Adapter layer or consumer database migration.

No Agent OS source/planning edits, Composer-lock changes, dependency reordering, consumer product/email-change wiring,
another queue or consumer-owned retry/terminalization state machine are included. Agent OS `TASK-00024` is an external
context ID, not an AccessControl TASK or dependency edge; neither its completion nor adoption is claimed.

Legacy bridges, historical readers/migrations/backfills, old-release backports, destructive resets, cryptographic key
operations, general corruption repair and new reliable-publication/audit-retention infrastructure are excluded. Do not
reopen completed original delivery work or EPIC-00009. Commit/push/PR publication, merge, signing/tagging, package
publication and deployment require their own authority.
