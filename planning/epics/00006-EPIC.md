# Recoverable Credential Delivery

## Destination

Publish a framework-neutral `v0.3.0` AccessControl contract that makes invitation, password-reset, and email-change
delivery recoverable from package-owned durable state. Every credential effect is claimed and committed before an
external provider call, records a typed outcome in a later expected-state transaction, and makes no exactly-once
claim.

## Decisions so far

- [WF-009](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-009-package-owned-credential-delivery-queue.md) makes existing grant-owned
  delivery state the only durable queue. A consumer outbox is not required or authoritative.
- [WF-010](../wayfinder/tickets/WF-010-retry-and-terminal-delivery-policy.md) selects retry-until-grant-expiry,
  package-owned bounded increasing backoff, and terminal ciphertext destruction for permanent failure.
- Stable delivery-generation identity is the provider idempotency identity. Post-commit events are optional immediate
  coordination, never recovery authority.
