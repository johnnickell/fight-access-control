# Reject incompatible credential-operation cohorts

## What to Build

Deliver persisted contract-version and required-capability enforcement across replacement issuance, lifecycle,
delivery/recovery and material-maintenance paths. Unsupported or incompatible composition rejects mutation/admission
rather than falling back to legacy behavior. This is slice B approved on 2026-09-27 under
[TICKET-00014](../tickets/00014-TICKET.md), targeting `v0.5.0`.

Include package Domain/Application compatibility contracts, public-path guards and behavioral conformance tests,
plus consumer cohort-switch guidance. Consumers own storage/trusted-boundary access fencing and deployment. New-code
checks cannot prevent an old binary that never executes them from writing; do not advertise that guarantee.

## Use Case and Contract

- **Actor/trigger:** an integrator composes/restarts a consumer cohort; any worker or maintainer attempts a credential
  mutation or sensitive admission against persisted contract state.
- **Validation:** validate required repository, transaction/authorization, cipher and protected-sink capabilities and
  persisted contract compatibility at the relevant boundaries. Document compatibility/version representations and
  transactional expected-state rules. Readiness is not a cached allow decision or a replacement for current authority.
- **Effects:** unsupported/missing/unknown state denies before mutation or materialization. Supported calls retain
  their owning service's atomic state/audit/result/event contract. No general deployment Command or new business Event
  is introduced; safe capability diagnostics cannot reveal secrets or arbitrary infrastructure exceptions.
- **Cohort:** one controlled compatible writer set agrees on supported persistence and new-key canonical creation
  versions. Retained historical readers remain distinct from the chosen creation version. TASK-00057 exercises
  historical semantics through this contract rather than weakening cohort checks for old requests.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Inventory every issuance, rotation/revocation, claim/admission/outcome, recovery, maintenance and relevant
      authority writer, including direct non-HTTP calls. Define supported capabilities, version compatibility and
      persisted checks without prescribing SQL/schema or adding a production Adapter layer.
- [ ] Missing contract storage, absent/incompatible capability, unknown persisted version and incompatible restarted
      worker reject mutation/admission without partial state, secret access, new-key fallback or legacy raw return.
      A transient unavailable store remains unavailable/indeterminate as appropriate, not proof of compatibility.
- [ ] Exercise actual provision/rotation, revocation, discovery-to-delivery, outcome and maintenance paths under
      compatible and incompatible composition. Capability checks from TASK-00046/00051 remain enforced; this slice
      adds cohort/version qualification, not a second authorization or lifecycle implementation.
- [ ] Fence version/compatibility decisions with the relevant transaction and writer state. Interleave cohort change
      with live work and reject stale authority; a successful startup check alone cannot authorize later admission.
      Already-admitted calls retain the fixed-sink inert-effect rules; deployment cannot recall disclosed bytes.
- [ ] Supported creation-version agreement and retained-reader obligations are explicit and testable. New creators
      cannot silently disagree about canonical rules or destination meaning. Historical requests remain subject to
      recorded versions and current authorization; TASK-00057 owns cross-version equivalence/conflict tests.
- [ ] Document quiescing all writers, draining/fencing admitted calls, preserving envelopes, installing compatible
      shared-connection capabilities and enabling discovery/admission only after qualification and key/sink readiness.
      Mixed legacy/new writers remain unsupported; no zero-downtime or automatic compatibility bridge is promised.
- [ ] Consumer-bindable scenarios require storage or trusted-boundary fencing of old binaries and real authority-
      writer races. Controlled package fixtures can prove the new guards, not actual old-binary access revocation.
      Record that external obligation explicitly; no composition boolean or documentation assertion qualifies it.
- [ ] M2 focused tests assert durable no-write/no-materialization outcomes and safe diagnostics. Current caller policy,
      finite defaults, capacity-preserved recovery and scoped publication-warning behavior remain unchanged. A deployment
      compatibility check is not an extra human approval step for routine operation/recovery.
