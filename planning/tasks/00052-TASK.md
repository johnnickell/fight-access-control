# Discover and recover interrupted Agent deliveries

## What to Build

Deliver bounded authorized due-work discovery and restart recovery for pending work, due retries and expired claims.
Reconcile lost sink responses and uncertain outcomes with the original delivery ID and bytes through TASK-00051's
actual claim/admission/invocation path. This is slice C approved on 2026-09-27 under
[TICKET-00013](../tickets/00013-TICKET.md), targeting `v0.5.0`.

Include safe Query/QueryHandler and Domain repository selection contracts, exact recovery commands/services,
receipt reconciliation, focused restart tests and scheduler composition guidance. Consumers schedule public work;
they do not copy claim, admission, retry or terminalization policy into a worker.

## Use Case and Contract

- **Actor/trigger:** an authenticated currently delegated scheduler starts/restarts or a worker recovers a lost
  invocation/outcome response. Retain original caller scope; worker identity is its own, not caller impersonation.
- **Queries:** deterministic bounded secret-free discovery/status for pending, due-retry and expired-claim state,
  enforcing current scope/delegation before existence disclosure. Reads do not mutate, commit or emit events.
  Reuse TASK-00046's shared safe outcome representation; TASK-00047 owns the separate public operation query.
- **Recovery:** acquire a new fenced claim/admission when required, resolve authoritative persisted admission/outcome,
  and reconcile exact durable receipts before unnecessary materialization where supported. An unconfirmed admission
  never decrypts/invokes. Absent/expired admission requires fresh valid authorization, not reused process memory.
- **Outcome:** confirmed already-completed work returns safe status without redelivery; rolled-back outcome can
  reconcile under current authority; unresolved storage remains indeterminate/unavailable, not delivered or absent
  permission to reissue. Any delivery fact follows its committed outcome; events never drive recovery authority.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A restarted default-only scheduler discovers pending work, due retry and abandoned expired claims without
      issuance events, initiating caller retry or process-local state. Dispatch invokes the real package delivery
      path. Equal due times have deterministic bounded ordering; queries are read-only and safe under invalid inputs.
- [ ] Wrong scope/caller, revoked permission/delegation and denied destination disclose no unauthorized work/status.
      Discovery authorization cannot stand in for the fresh admission checks. Worker delegation preserves original scope.
- [ ] Receipt-accepted/lost-response and both committed/rolled-back admission and outcome uncertainty recover honestly
      after restart. Current claims may reconcile exact receipts; stale/expired/replaced claimants cannot acknowledge.
      Test competing retries, crash before/after each durable boundary and claim takeover while old invocation is in flight.
- [ ] Sink retries are at-least-once with unchanged delivery ID, original bytes and reserved slot order despite new
      attempt tokens/fences. No new Agent, generation, rotation or issuance/audit fact occurs. Confirmed completion
      stops delivery; invalid receipts cannot complete. No fallback through authentication-envelope decryption.
- [ ] Temporary sink/key failure retains bounded ciphertext/key version and safe retry classification. Restored access
      delivers original material. Expiry/permanent failure remains terminal; retired work cannot be reclaimed to revive
      authority. Delivered-secret loss yields safe reconciliation, not a new secret read or automatic rotation.
- [ ] Document/test finite batch/work/retry scheduling defaults and optional validated overrides, with claim/admission,
      retention and authorization deadline relationships. Invalid overrides reject; retries remain bounded. New-work
      capacity does not block existing status/recovery. No manual limits configuration or extra human approval is needed
      for routine recovery; unavailable storage is not promised to succeed.
- [ ] Safe query/results, serialization/debug, audit/events and failure classifications contain no raw/ciphertext secret,
      key path or provider diagnostic. Receipt reconciliation is not delivery-material retrieval or authority to activate,
      launch or sign. Post-admission late effects remain inert under TASK-00051's protected sink contract.
- [ ] Focused package tests prove TICKET-00013 D1/D3 and relevant D2/D6/D7. TASK-00049 owns the combined provision-and-
      rotation scenario with both issuance publishers failing and caller termination; this slice supplies the real
      scheduler path, not a test-only simulation. Its own restart tests cannot be deferred to TASK-00049 or TASK-00054.
- [ ] Document safe composition, retryable/terminal/indeterminate classifications and consumer obligations without
      selecting a queue, cron framework, production adapter or transport. Update owning acceptance evidence references.
