## Use Case and Contract

- **Actor/trigger:** a consumer maintainer restores stale current-contract data after possible mutation or external
  acceptance; a worker/caller then tries issuance, retry, delivery admission or maintenance against restored state.
- **Validation:** current authorization and compatible cohort are necessary but insufficient. Define authoritative
  reconciliation/readiness evidence, its trusted boundary and fence against concurrent writers before enabling effects.
  A flag restored with the stale database cannot independently prove that external history matches it.
- **Effects/results:** under the supported integration contract, unreconciled/missing/unknown evidence keeps unsafe
  mutation/admission unavailable, not silently successful. Reconciled compatible state retains original operation and
  delivery identities, canonical semantics, slot order and terminal evidence. No new migration business Event or
  fabricated issuance/receipt is introduced; ordinary public operations retain their own atomic results/events.
- **Limit:** the package cannot automatically detect arbitrary database rollback or fence an old binary by running
  new-code checks. Consumers must establish trusted storage/admission fencing and reconcile external state; unsupported
  integrations remain unsupported rather than receiving a package-only safety claim.
