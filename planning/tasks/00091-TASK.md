### Actors, Triggers and Side Effects

| Actor and trigger | Commands / Queries / Events | Outcome and effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trusted producer records or retries | Common RecordNotice; existing NoticeRecorded after new acknowledged commit | Current independent recording admission before identity lookup; preserve deduplication/conflict and post-commit behavior. |
| Authorized caller removes exact retained targets | Common RemoveNotices; no removal event | Independently admit every target and retained record before deletion; preserve actual atomic removal/loss evidence. |
| Background producer/cleanup worker reaches a protected step | TASK-00088 authority admission, then owning Common operation | Recheck original credential, current Permissions and consumer scope without nonce replay or credential switching. |

Permission checks create no business message/event/grant. Authentication nonce consumption, Common's no-change storage
lock UPDATE and actual notice mutations are distinct effects. Topic, recipient, author, subject and causation references
are protected policy inputs, not credentials. Human/operator rights never implicitly become Agent authority.
