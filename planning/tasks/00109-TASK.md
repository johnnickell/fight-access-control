# Organize Application capabilities and descriptive collaborator names

## What to Build

Deliver the cohesion/naming slice of [TICKET-00033](../tickets/00033-TICKET.md). Organize real collaborators by
reasons to change and make dependency names identify their capability. Normally one mechanical/refactoring PR;
no new use cases, semantic normalization, broad forwarding layer or persistence abstraction.

### Actors, Contracts and Effects

Maintainers/integrators locate and compose existing authentication, provisioning, rotation/revocation, delivery,
maintenance, continuation, permission assignment, Feature discovery and authorization admission capabilities.
Existing Commands/Queries/Events, input/results, grants and side effects remain unchanged. Namespace presence and
constructor injection are neither execution nor authorization.

### Scope

  orchestration/result and test near its owner without one-class folders or moving transport presentation back inward.

  normal bus routing, transaction ownership and post-commit publication visible. Share actual assignment knowledge,
  not a generic service facade or one abstraction per private method.
- Group FeatureReferences, FeatureReferenceScope, FeatureDiscoveryResult and FeatureReferenceDiscovery under Feature
  Discovery; group declaration/admission collaborators under a meaningful authorization Admission home.
- Inspect all project-owned PHP source, tests, shipped schema helpers and maintained runnable examples for ambiguous
  repository/bus/dispatcher collaborators and correct them. Examples: $agents → $agentRepository,
  $operations → $agentOperationRepository, $features → $featureRepository, $queries → $queryBus,
  $commands → $synchronousCommandBus, $events → $eventDispatcher. Use actual current types after other refactors.
- Correct ambiguous locals/parameters when their role is the collaborator; retain readable $users/$roles for actual
  collections and distinguish generic trusted capabilities by domain intent rather than blindly copying type names.
- Update named-argument callers, registrations/reflection, autoload/static checks, mirrored tests and maintained
  composition docs/CONTEXT/README/Unreleased notes together. Do not rewrite released history or add legacy aliases.
- Preserve existing Clock/generator/cipher ownership behind inward contracts and the approved naming rules. No
  Common/provider/consumer file edits or new framework dependencies are authorized by this source refactor.
