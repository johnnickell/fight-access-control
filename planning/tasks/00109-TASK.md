# Organize Application capabilities and descriptive collaborator names

## What to Build

Deliver the cohesion/naming slice of [TICKET-00033](../tickets/00033-TICKET.md). Organize real collaborators by
reasons to change and make dependency names identify their capability. Normally one mechanical/refactoring PR;
no new use cases, semantic normalization, broad forwarding layer or persistence abstraction.

### Actors, Contracts and Effects

Maintainers/integrators locate and compose existing authentication, provisioning, rotation/revocation, delivery,
maintenance, continuation, permission assignment, Feature discovery and authorization admission capabilities.

constructor injection are neither execution nor authorization.
