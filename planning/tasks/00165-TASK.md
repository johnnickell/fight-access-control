  containment/primary fault and exact authorization epoch/deadline/revision checks remain; no new Event/publication
  machinery or extension of issuance's warning-return exception.

  references. No audit actor/status/global count grants cleanup, physical key retirement, delivery, activation or use.
  Recheck trusted time after slow key/commit work; optional audit weakens none of the cohort/restoration/all-writer,
  destination, key-reference, CAS, cancellation or retention safeguards.
- Evidence/context is bounded and action-specific, excluding raw secret/key/ciphertext/hashes, sink payloads, messages/
