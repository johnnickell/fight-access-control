canonical Common APIs, establish the sufficient minimum, and give remaining feature owners a precise contract

### Actors, Messages and Effects

profile, Resource/Skill, authority and native behavior. Existing Commands/Queries/Events and effects are unchanged.
Future features use actual Common handlers/buses and ports, not AccessControl copies/aliases. Missing supplier
