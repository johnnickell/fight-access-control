- Replace unexplained nullable status Views with minimal explicit typed absence/state contracts. Repositories retain

- Check requested User, purpose, delivery generation and applicable grant/delivery relationships at their owner.
  A wrong returned identity/purpose is an internal contract fault where exactness is promised, not not-found or
  successful unrelated status. Do not duplicate native return-type checks or rely on unchecked result assertions.
- Validate each repository's batch element/purpose, promised time/filter/order and maximum size BEFORE merging.
  An individual source returning excessive or inconsistent work rejects; do not hide it through global truncation.
- Preserve legitimate cross-family merge, deterministic ordering/ties and global top-N selection. Three valid
  individually bounded source batches may legitimately exceed the final limit when combined; selecting the global
  limit is not the faulty-page truncation prohibited by the parent ticket. Do not force all sources into one budget.
- Preserve the actual admitted read time, expiry/due boundaries and existing authority/recovery eligibility. Validate
  only promised facts available to this boundary; do not add stronger snapshot semantics, unbounded scans or new locks.
- Operational/internal faults cannot become empty work, ordinary status absence or diagnostic-derived business
  categories. Preserve existing authorization/concealment and safe public projection; Domain does not depend on
  Application failures. Directly affected public schema/example alignment is included without re-owning TICKET-00041.
- All four Queries remain read-only: no claim, expiration, material cleanup, recovery admission, audit insertion,
  transaction/commit or Event dispatch. Results are advisory and do not replace a Command's final state/authority checks.
