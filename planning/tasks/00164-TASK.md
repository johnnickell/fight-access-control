## Contracts and Boundaries

- Give the actual delivery evidence owner an optional last nullable AuditEvidenceRepository; omitted/null intentionally
  disables durable audit. Configured failure cannot silently disable/discard it. Recovery must not acquire a duplicate
  audit writer or an independent transaction merely to call the public delivery path.
- Consume TASK-00162's retained authoritative originating provenance through restart, not a label parsed from scope,
  current worker/epoch, target Agent or public View. The real delegated worker still authenticates/authorizes itself
  under current scope/target/destination fences; originating attribution neither impersonates that caller nor grants
  authority. No ambient resolver or System/Anonymous fallback for missing origin. Validate attribution before enabled
  mutations requiring evidence; disabled/pure resolution paths gain no new audit-only input, and existing required
  business provenance/admission stays required. Admit necessary provenance without sending first and only then
  discovering that a known missing attribution makes required recording impossible.
- Explicitly insert evidence in each actual transaction/connection that commits a meaningful delivery outcome, using
  typed Agent/credential/operation/delivery relationships, scoped original key/correlation, applicable before/after
  operation revisions and the trusted outcome time used for that transition. Do not use claim/admission timestamps
  or message time as a later outcome timestamp, inspect Events, or dump issuance/receipt/request payloads.
- Audit confirmed delivery and committed retryable/terminal failure, plus real expiry/exhaustion transitions. Current
  claimDelivery can itself expire material or terminalize exhausted attempts; record those meaningful committed
  outcomes in their owning transaction. Ordinary temporary claims/admissions/polls produce no mutation evidence merely
  for advancing internal state. Recorded-result/receipt reconciliation without a state change creates no duplicate
  evidence; unavailable/deferred/rejected work is not fabricated committed failure evidence.
- Preserve separate claim/admission/outcome transactions, exact expected attempt/token/revision/epoch and Agent/
  operation/cohort/restoration/destination fences. Keep protected sink invocation outside every database transaction.
  Audit failure rolls back required staged state/evidence but cannot undo a send/stage that already occurred.
  Receipt-first restart and retained original bytes/IDs reconcile uncertainty without blind restaging/new issuance.
- Preserve both uncertain outcomes at each commit, safe INDETERMINATE/UNAVAILABLE/REJECTED/DEFERRED and confirmed result
  distinctions, current delivery-loss reconciliation and primary fault containment without exposing provider causes.
  A late/stale acknowledgement cannot revive cancelled/expired/rewrapped state. Do not add new Events, publication
  promises, issuance-style warning returns, outbox/replay/deduplication framework or rejected-attempt durability.
- Recovery keeps one bounded authorized selection/pass, independent results and polling without Event/caller-retry
  dependence. Status/due discovery remains read-only and grants no claim/materialization/admission/activation authority.
  Follow actual configured dispatch adopted by TASK-00128/00134/00144; do not recreate a direct-handler bypass, shadow
  bus or generic-inference assertion as part of audit. This slice proves audit/recovery behavior, not missing bus APIs.
- Evidence/context must be bounded action-specific admitted facts, excluding raw credentials, keys, hashes/ciphertext,
  invocation/sink payloads, entities/messages, diagnostics and unnecessary personal data. Typed evidence is not public/
  log-safe automatically; free text needs explicit admission. Keep invocation material sensitive/non-serializable.
