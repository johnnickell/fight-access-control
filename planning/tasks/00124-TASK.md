# Audit invitation delivery and expiry

## What to Build

Deliver optional explicit atomic audit for DeliverUserInvitation, ResendInvitationDelivery, RetryInvitationDelivery
and ExpireInvitationDelivery and their corresponding handlers under
[TICKET-00044](../tickets/00044-TICKET.md). Record meaningful committed delivery, failure, replacement and expiry
outcomes with the shared evidence contract, preserving the existing claim/send/outcome recovery boundary.

### Actors, Messages and Effects

- Trusted delivery workers and authorized resend/retry callers supply explicit typed originating attribution.
  Preserve an admitted User/Agent through worker execution; deliberate autonomous expiry/maintenance may establish
  SystemId, never as missing-data fallback. Actor identity is not entry authorization or target authority.
- Own the four Commands/handlers' necessary constructor/input/Event adaptations, canonical current round trips,
  callers, test fixtures/assertions and current worker/composition guidance. Creation/correction of pending Users
  belongs to TASK-00123; synchronous activation belongs to TASK-00127.
- Retain existing success/failure publication and read-only status/discovery contracts. No new audit Query, scheduler,
  transport route, permission grant or automatically reliable event pipeline is introduced.

### Behavior and Validation

- Use an optional last nullable AuditEvidenceRepository argument on each handler. Omitted/null intentionally means
  no durable-audit guarantee; configured persistence failure must not silently disable or discard required evidence.
  Preserve business-required inputs; add optional provenance only where necessary and absent under the parent policy.
- Enabled auditing validates required attribution before the first mutation requiring evidence. Do not add a record
  for a poll/internal claim merely to demand actor input sooner. Disabled/pure no-change paths gain no new audit-only
  obligation; existing authorization, identity and generation/reference checks remain.
- Add evidence directly within the actual transaction committing its meaningful outcome, not through repository/
  event inspection or after publication. Use typed User/grant/delivery references, trusted outcome occurrence time,
  actual applicable revisions and real message/delivery correlation. Preserve private-invitation/registration origin.
- Audit meaningful confirmed and committed failed-delivery transitions, resend/retry replacement outcomes and actual
  expiry changes. No evidence for unknown/stale/terminal/not-due deliveries, failed CAS/no-change paths or temporary
  claim/helper execution. Preserve validation and stale-writer defenses rather than treating no-change as unguarded.
- Keep separately committed claims, external provider calls and outcome persistence in their current order/boundary.
  Audit failure aborts required staged outcome state/evidence; it cannot unsend an external delivery or prove an old
  claim vanished. Preserve receipt/lease/generation recovery, primary failures and truthful uncertain outcomes.
- Expiry uses the actual admitted business occurrence time, preserving due-time, latest-grant, ownership and
  expected-state semantics. Add evidence only for a confirmed committed expiry, not stale discovery or a CAS loser.
- Keep evidence durable if ordinary post-commit success/failure notification throws. No guaranteed rejected-attempt
  store, implicit retry/deduplication or scoped Agent issuance-warning behavior is added here.
- Exclude activation credentials, tokens, hashes, ciphertext/provider payloads, raw messages/entities/exception
  diagnostics and unnecessary personal data. Bounded context/reasons require explicit admission; no wholesale audit
  export into public responses/logs or claim that a string regex alone guarantees safety.
