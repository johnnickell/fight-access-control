  and precise Query/optional issuance/disposition inference. Negative fixtures check actual expected diagnostics for

  bypass, legacy service facade or local bus shim. Queries remain read-only through Domain repositories.
- TASK-00182 owns conversion, complete business behavior/basic bus proof, current callers/docs, full gate and independent

  conversion supplier as part of qualification. Existing 37 void paths and Query adoption retain their separate owners.
