### Actors, Triggers and Side Effects

| Actor and trigger | Commands / Queries / Events | Outcome and effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trusted producer issues supported requests; caller reads them | Owning Common issuance/state operations | Independently admit issuer and complete protected request disclosure; retain server-authored validation privately. |
| Agent submits partial/full input or confirmation | Owning Common input Command/method handling | Current caller/Task/operation/request/group/expiry binding and scope before protected validation and atomic admission. |
| Consumer observes accepted answers and reaches protected continuation | Owning Common observation/consumption contract; consumer work remains separate | Recheck current original authority/scope and single-use admission; acceptance is not execution approval or receipt. |

may precede protected validation; do not promise authorization before all request-shape parsing. Checks introduce
no business event/grant and replay no authentication nonce. Consumer decides destructiveness and actual effects.
