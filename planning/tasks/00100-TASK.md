### Actors, Triggers and Side Effects

| Actor and trigger | Commands / Queries / Events | Outcome and effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agent requests cancellation or repeats it | Owning Common cancellation Command/method | Independent current cancellation/scope admission, durable intent before initial acknowledgment, qualified repeat/terminal no-op semantics. |
| Execution owner observes retained intent | Owning Common authorized intent/state Query | Separate current observation permission; read is neither consumption nor proof of stopping. |
| Input/producer transition races cancellation; consumer continues or stops | Actual Common input/transition/continuation operations | Preserve accepted answers, consumed confirmation and terminal winner; consumer reports actual outcome independently. |

Checking adds no business message/event. Common owns durable intent/commit/safe facts where defined. Consumer
stopping remains a separate effect; acknowledgment, disconnect, TTL or removal cannot imply cancelled outcome,
worker observation, rollback, compensation or forced termination. Ordinary requests retain snapshot/nonce semantics.
