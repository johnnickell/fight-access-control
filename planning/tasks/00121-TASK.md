# Introduce fixed system and anonymous identities

## What to Build

Deliver the small independently verifiable identity prerequisite for
[TICKET-00043](../tickets/00043-TICKET.md) and [EPIC-00017](../epics/00017-EPIC.md): direct SystemId and AnonymousId
values using Common's actual existing UniqueId pattern, with the explicitly approved fixed-nil exception.
This introduces no audit orchestration and does not replace the evidence model or enable new handler audit paths.

### Scope and Observable Behavior

- SystemId represents deliberate autonomous system work; AnonymousId represents a deliberately unauthenticated
  initiator. They are different types even though both serialize to Uuid::NIL. No User/Agent account, login,
  per-service identity registry, singleton or AuditActor wrapper is required.
- Only Uuid::NIL is valid. generate() returns the fixed nil identity rather than a fresh identifier;
  fromString(Uuid::NIL) succeeds. Every supported construction/hydration path rejects non-nil or malformed values
  rather than coercing them to nil, generating an identity or selecting a fallback actor.
- Extend supported Common Domain primitives, preserve inward dependency direction and place values/tests under
  their appropriate Domain ownership using current project naming. Do not introduce a new generic identity
  hierarchy or namespace/framework solely to hold two special identities.
- Verify canonical string round trips, equality/hash behavior and preservation of actor kind through the supported
  value reconstruction contract. SystemId and AnonymousId must not compare equal to one another or to a UserId/
  AgentId carrying identical UUID text. Do not impose a new cross-type ordering contract on Common.
- Document the deliberate fixed-nil exception to ordinary fresh identity generation. An audit evidence record's
  identity remains unique/non-nil and is separately owned by TASK-00122; do not reuse these IDs for evidence.
- Attribution confers no authority. Preserve the distinction between original admitted User/Agent provenance and
  autonomous system execution; missing actor information is not permission to use either special identity.

### Ownership and Boundaries

This is the approved small prefactor before the coherent evidence/caller replacement in
[TASK-00122](00122-TASK.md). It owns the two identities, direct validation/tests and current identity guidance.
TASK-00122 owns the evolved evidence contract, actor-kind persistence representation and necessary writer/message/
port adaptations. Do not change existing writers, constructors or outcome coverage merely to demonstrate usage.

Actor examples are trusted autonomous provisioning/maintenance and declared anonymous public requests. No new
Command, Query, Event, transport route, permission grant or persistence adapter is required in this prefactor.
Shared optional audit wiring/admission and expanded family outcomes remain with the approved later owners; this
small identity change is not proof that optional auditing or Feature provisioning adoption is implemented.
