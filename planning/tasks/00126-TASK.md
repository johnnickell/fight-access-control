### Behavior and Validation

- Add optional last nullable AuditEvidenceRepository injection everywhere owned, including current mandatory
  writers. Omitted/null means intentionally disabled and no durable-audit promise; configured write failure never
  enables silent no-op/discard behavior. Preserve existing business-required actors and policy checks.
- Supply direct typed attribution, reusing adequate existing actor fields. Where new audit-only input is needed,
  make it optional and reject missing provenance before the first mutation requiring enabled evidence. Disabled/
  pure no-change execution gains no new audit-only requirement. No ambient actor resolution, label guessing or nil fallback.
- Audit meaningful committed self-service and administrative requests/cancellations, confirmed/committed failed
  delivery and actual expiry. Use explicit package actions and typed User/grant/delivery relationships, authoritative
  applicable before/after revisions, trusted outcome time and real message/operation correlation.
- Add evidence inside the actual owning transaction/connection that couples User reservation, grant and delivery
  state. Preserve email reservation uniqueness/revision, target authorization, canonical validation, old/new-email
  authority, grant replacement/single-use, latest work and expected-state/CAS/fencing safeguards.
- For expiry preserve current admitted occurrence/due time, latest issued grant, matching User reservation and
  coupled release/expiry semantics. Stale/not-due/terminal/failed-CAS and other validated no-change cases get no
  mutation record; no-change is not permission to skip ownership/reference admission. Do not record polls/internal claims.
- Audit failure before commit/concurrent loss rolls back required coupled state/evidence. Preserve separately
  committed claims/external sends/receipts and recovery truth: rollback cannot unsend a message. Preserve primary
  failures, uncertain commit and ordinary post-commit propagation; notification failure does not remove committed audit.
- Do not duplicate records from lower-level grant/session helpers or manufacture rejected-attempt evidence. No
  Agent issuance-warning exception, generic replay/deduplication or post-commit event-derived audit is added.
- Exclude email credentials/tokens/hashes/ciphertext, raw messages/entities/provider payloads, diagnostic exceptions
  and unnecessary personal data. Raw old/new addresses are not automatically safe audit context; admit only needed
  bounded facts under the shared field policy, including reasons. Do not publish/log evidence wholesale.
